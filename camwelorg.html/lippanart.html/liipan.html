ABOUT LIPPAN ART

Art That Reflects Our Culture

Lippan Art is a traditional Indian art form originating from the Kutch region of Gujarat. It uses clay, mud and small mirrors to create beautiful decorative patterns.

Each handmade creation brings a unique combination of tradition and modern beauty.
